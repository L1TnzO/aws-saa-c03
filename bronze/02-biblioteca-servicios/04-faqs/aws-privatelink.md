### What are the benefits of using a VPC endpoint with AWS PrivateLink?

VPC endpoints provide secure access to a specific service or resource, with several benefits to the end user:

- VPC endpoints provide access to a specific service or resource without the need of using any other gateways – no need to use an internet gateway, NAT gateway, VPN connection, or a VPC peering connection, reducing the risk of exposing your resources to the internet or other outside networks.
- Your traffic remains within Amazon's private network, reducing the risk of exposing your traffic to the internet.
- When accessing Amazon services over VPC endpoints, you can restrict the access through a VPC endpoint to specific users, actions, and/or resources.
- You can limit access to resources provided by an Amazon service to traffic originating from a specific VPC or through a specific VPC endpoint.
